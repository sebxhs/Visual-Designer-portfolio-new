Genmap Logo Design

Genmap is a logo design project focused on creating a strong visual foundation for a growing brand.

Project Overview

A flexible mark for digital and physical applications

The goal was to develop a logo that communicates the brand's identity while remaining flexible across digital and physical applications.

Design Process

From discovery to final identity development

The project involved:

  • Research and discovery
  • Concept sketching
  • Logo exploration
  • Refinement and testing
  • Final identity development

Final Result

Clean, scalable, and recognizable

The resulting logo is clean, scalable, and recognizable, providing the brand with a strong visual presence and a foundation for future growth.
