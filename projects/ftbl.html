FTBL Brand Identity

FTBL is a brand identity project inspired by the culture, emotion, and community surrounding football.

Project Overview

A flexible system for football culture

The challenge was to create a visual system that reflects the excitement of the sport while remaining flexible enough for digital, social, and merchandise applications.

Design Approach

Bold, dynamic, and consistent

The identity was built around:

  • Bold typography
  • Dynamic layouts
  • High-impact visuals
  • Strong brand consistency
  • Scalable design systems

Outcome

A memorable and versatile brand experience

The final identity successfully captures the energy of football culture while creating a memorable and versatile brand experience.
